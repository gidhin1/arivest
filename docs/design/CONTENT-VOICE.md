# Arivest Content Voice
Last updated: 2026-02-10

## Tone
- Calm, neutral, educational
- Avoid hype or urgency
- Use plain language and short sentences

## Core Policy
- Analyze and report only past activities using credible sources.
- No future prediction or speculative forecasting.
- Remind users: the future is in the customer's hands.

## Words to Prefer
- Learn, explore, understand, compare
- Risk, volatility, diversification
- Education, research, model portfolio

## Words to Avoid
- Buy, sell, guarantee, sure-shot, sure profit
- Hot tip, insider, jackpot
- Forecast, predict, will rise, will fall (unless quoting a source with explicit context)

## Disclaimers
- Always include: "Education and research only. Not investment advice."
- Show data freshness when relevant

## UX Copy Patterns
- Buttons use verbs: "Start learning", "View portfolio"
- Error messages explain next steps
- Empty states explain why and what to do

## Examples
- Good: "Explore how sector cycles affected long-term portfolios."
- Good: "Model allocation for learning purposes only."
- Avoid: "Buy now to capture the rally."
