# Arivest Research Policy
Last updated: 2026-02-10

## Core Principle
Arivest analyzes and reports only on past activities using credible, verifiable sources. We do not provide future predictions or speculative forecasts. The future is in the customer's hands.

## What This Means
- All research content must be historical, sourced, and time-stamped.
- No content may imply guaranteed outcomes or predictive certainty.
- Model portfolios are educational examples, not future predictions.

## Enforcement
- Every research item must include sources and an as-of date.
- Editorial review rejects any forward-looking or speculative language.
- Product experiences must avoid buy/sell language and hype framing.

## Disclosure
“Education and research only. Not investment advice.”
